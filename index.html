Program Spotlight - TRACE District-Wide Hiring Audit

San Diego CCD is launching TRACE, a first-of-its-kind district-wide audit of all hiring processes guided by a cross-constituent EEO Workgroup that mirrors the District Governance Council. The project disaggregates five years of hiring data, conducts six process-mapping workshops and twelve focus groups across all four colleges and four continuing education sites, and publishes a White Paper of evidence-based recommendations — anonymous resume screening, structured rubrics, and inclusive outreach — for Governing Board adoption and pilot implementation.

GOAL 1Inclusive Governance Structure

Establish an inclusive governance structure to guide the hiring-practices audit and ensure every constituency has an equal vote.

Activities

  • Convene a cross-constituent EEO Workgroup mirroring the District Governance Council (DGC).
  • Invite the Academic Senate, Classified Senate, Student Senate, CSEA, Equity Office, Institutional Research, and college HR leads.
  • Host a kick-off retreat to review the Committee Charge, roles, decision-making protocols, and timeline.
  • Provide stipends or release time; circulate agendas and pre-reads at least one week prior.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    Workgroup formed with each constituency holding an equal vote and recommending authority.
    AssessmentMembership roster; charter adoption.
  2. 02
    At least 80% attendance at bi-monthly full-committee meetings.
    AssessmentAttendance logs and pre-read receipts tracked by II&E.
GOAL 2Comprehensive Hiring Audit

Complete a comprehensive hiring-practices audit across all four colleges and the district office.

Activities

  • Map and document hiring workflows at all four colleges and the district office.
  • Disaggregate and analyze five years of hiring data (applicant demographics, pass-rates, time-to-hire, offer-declines).
  • Capture qualitative insights through six process-mapping workshops and twelve focus groups (recent hires, hiring managers, union reps).

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    Baseline EEO metrics established by Month 6 (applicant diversity, screening variances, time-to-hire, offer-acceptance).
    AssessmentBaseline Metrics Report validated in a data-review workshop.
  2. 02
    “Barrier” themes identified and validated with the full Workgroup.
    AssessmentThematic coding of workshops & focus groups; synthesis session.
GOAL 3Evidence-Based Recommendations

Generate evidence-based recommendations, synthesized into a rigorously vetted White Paper.

Activities

  • Synthesize quantitative trends and qualitative themes into a White Paper.
  • Identify prioritized interventions — anonymous resume screening, structured rubrics, inclusive outreach — each with cost estimates and projected impact.
  • Commission a literature review and peer-district scan of best-in-class practices.
  • Post a draft executive summary for a districtwide public comment period.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    White Paper published with a prioritized set of evidence-based recommendations.
    AssessmentMid-point workshop; public comment incorporated.
  2. 02
    Implementation roadmap with phased pilot timeline and evaluation checkpoints.
    AssessmentRoadmap reviewed and approved by the Workgroup.
GOAL 4Pilot High-Impact Interventions

Design and implement high-impact pilot interventions in selected faculty and classified searches.

Activities

  • Scope and launch pilots such as anonymous resume reviews and standardized interviewer training.
  • Develop quick-start guides and training; appoint “pilot champions” in each area.
  • Monitor pilot efficacy via predefined success metrics and tracking dashboards.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    Increase in underrepresented finalists per search and a reduction in time-to-hire disparities.
    AssessmentPilot data compared to baseline in a mid-pilot evaluation report.
  2. 02
    Positive participant satisfaction with pilot processes.
    AssessmentPost-pilot participant surveys.
GOAL 5Governing Board Adoption

Secure Governing Board adoption of the White Paper and pilot protocols.

Activities

  • Present the finalized White Paper and pilot evaluation results to the SDCCD Governing Board.
  • Work with the Board liaison to draft a formal resolution.
  • Share a publicly accessible one-pager and slide deck.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    Formal Board resolution endorsing all Tier 1 recommendations.
    AssessmentAdopted Board resolution on record.
  2. 02
    Board presentation delivered within the grant timeline.
    AssessmentMeeting minutes and presentation materials.
GOAL 6Institutionalize & Sustain

Institutionalize and sustain best practices beyond the grant period.

Activities

  • Embed approved interventions into the EEO Plan, HR policy manuals, and SOPs by June 30, 2028.
  • Stand up three sub-committees: Data & Metrics; Policy & Procedure; Training & Development.
  • Establish quarterly check-ins tied to NOVA data reporting deadlines.

Measurable Outcomes & Assessment

  1. 01
    At least five new policies or procedures integrated into the EEO Plan and HR manuals.
    AssessmentUpdated EEO Plan / HR policy documents.
  2. 02
    Standing sub-committees chartered and reporting annually via NOVA EEO metrics.
    AssessmentSub-committee charters; annual EEO Hiring Report.
How the goals connect
Application Narrative · Section 1.3

Together, these six goals form a cohesive, research-supported path from diagnostic audit to sustained equity in SDCCD’s hiring practices. Inclusive governance and a five-year data audit diagnose systemic barriers; an evidence-based White Paper and high-impact pilots test solutions; and a Governing Board resolution plus standing sub-committees institutionalize the gains — addressing the gap between SDCCD’s diverse student population and the demographics of its faculty, staff, and leadership.

Why This Project
Problem statement & project scope, Sections 1.1 – 2.2

SDCCD serves one of the most diverse student bodies in California, yet faculty, staff, and leadership demographics do not fully reflect it. Climate-survey feedback and exit-interview data point to opaque decision-making, inconsistent screening rubrics, and unstructured interview panels — uneven hiring outcomes across colleges. TRACE diagnoses these systemic barriers and pilots transparent, equity-centered solutions.

Workplan Activities
Activity clusters A–H · from Section 2.2
A – D · Diagnose
  • EEO Workgroup mobilization. Formal invitations to the District Governance Council; kick-off retreat and charter.
  • Quantitative data collection & analysis. Pull five years of hiring data with II&E and HRIS; present as interactive dashboards (Power BI).
  • Qualitative process mapping & focus groups. Campus-based workshops plus twelve focus groups with recent hires, hiring managers, and union reps.
  • Benchmarking & literature review. Peer-district scan of best-in-class practices reviewed by sub-committees.
E – H · Act & Sustain
  • White Paper drafting & validation. Synthesize findings; mid-point workshop; public comment period.
  • Pilot design & implementation. Anonymous resume screening and standardized interviewer training with “pilot champions.”
  • Board presentation & adoption. Finalize the White Paper; secure a formal resolution endorsing Tier 1 recommendations.
  • Sustainability & transition. Update the EEO Plan and HR manuals; quarterly check-ins tied to NOVA reporting deadlines.
Institutional Resources Used
  • Office of Institutional Innovation & Effectiveness (II&E). Research & Planning Analysts, Power BI dashboards, NOVA submissions, and five-year hiring-trend analysis.
  • People & Culture / HRIS. Comprehensive HRIS tracking applicant, hiring-manager, and position data; EEO compliance reporting for the Multiple Methods model.
  • Participatory governance. Governance Handbook and council system — Academic, Classified, and Student Senates, and the District Council on Diversity & Equity.
  • Equity & training resources. Equal Opportunity & Diversity Office and the Professional Learning & Leadership Development Center.
  • Existing EEO Plan & standing EEO Advisory Committee. A foundation of policies, prior assessments, and stakeholder relationships.
Three Standing Sub-Committees

To sustain the work beyond the grant, pilot groups transition into three permanent sub-committees under the District Council on Diversity & Equity:

  • Data & Metrics. Reviews quarterly NOVA metrics, adjusts dashboards, advises HR on data trends.
  • Policy & Procedure. Updates EEO policies, maintains the EEO Plan, ensures inclusive language in job postings.
  • Training & Development. Leads “train-the-trainer” programs on rubric use, bias mitigation, and equity-focused data literacy.
